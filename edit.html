THIS EVE LIFE · TEL-Manual Editor

manual.json is filled from the Discord calendar every 15 minutes by the TEL-Manual feed run, so this editor is only for when you want to write the list yourself. Nothing here touches the VPS or the automatic set. Fill in the shows, press Build JSON, copy it, and paste it over manual.json on GitHub. The overlays pick it up within about 5 minutes (GitHub Pages cache); right-click → Refresh in OBS to hurry it.
